## 2. Global rules

- Fixture identity: exact tuple `provider_season_id + fixture_id + ordered home_team_id + ordered away_team_id`. Display names and context columns are never join keys.
- Chronology: all historical evidence must be strictly earlier than the runtime fixture kickoff. Same-kickoff and post-kickoff records are excluded.
- Settled match: status must be in the builder's explicit settled-status allowlist and both scores must be valid non-negative integers.
- Missing evidence: preserve explicit status/reason such as `NOT_AVAILABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `NO_VERIFIED_PRIOR_H2H`, `BLOCKED`, or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`; never silently impute.
- Schedule disagreement: exact identity may pass while kickoff differs. Keep both times and signed delta; require manual authoritative review. Never auto-correct.
- Production: raw supported Oracle probabilities are copied unchanged. Research-only features cannot alter them.
- Eligibility: all Selected-50 report rows remain `production_eligible=false` and `betting_eligible=false`.
- Confidence, accuracy, calibration, A+ and value claims remain unavailable until their independent evidence gates pass.

## 5. Supporting-tab field definitions

### xG Evidence Audit
- Fixture Date / League / Fixture: display-only context.
- Fixture identity fields: exact provider season, fixture ID and ordered home/away team IDs.
- Raw xG source fields: values copied from the pinned archive; retain source field name, archive source file/row and raw-record hash.
- Numeric xG values: parsed numeric values only; no inference from actual-match xG.
- Capture timestamp / revision provenance: evidence that the value was available before target kickoff and that source revisions are understood.
- Eligibility and quarantine: all rows remain quarantined and prediction/betting ineligible until timestamp and revision gates pass.

### H2H Intelligence
- One row per exact prior meeting.
- Includes target fixture context, target fixture ID/selected ID, target history cutoff, H2H scope, provider competition ID, meeting rank, historical fixture ID/kickoff/season, ordered historical team IDs, score, current-home-perspective result and total goals.
- H2H scope uses explicit provider competition ID where present; otherwise exact season fallback. Cross-season H2H is not verified merely because a team name matches.

### Team Form Evidence
- One row per target fixture and team side.
- Overall last-five sample count, form string, GF/GA averages, points per game and historical empirical rates.
- Venue split is home-at-home for the home side and away-away for the away side.
- Source fixture IDs must be retained so each aggregate can be reconstructed.
- Historical empirical rates are descriptive outcomes from the sample, not predicted probabilities.

### Data Quality & Risk
- Exact fixture identity, join status, schedule status/reason, runtime/archive kickoff and signed delta, history cutoff, home/away evidence risk, sample sizes, H2H scope, archive source file/row/hash and production/betting eligibility.
- Exact join success and schedule match are separate checks.
- Eligibility must remain NO for both production and betting.

### Schedule Reconciliation
- One row per kickoff discordance.
- Required: fixture context, status/reason, exact identity, selected league ID, provider season, ordered home/away team IDs, runtime kickoff, archive kickoff, signed archive-minus-runtime delta, archive source file/row, exact-join status and manual resolution action.
- No automated timestamp selection or correction.

### Goal Intelligence
- One row per fixture/team side with overall and venue sample sizes, form, GF/GA and total-goals averages, historical BTTS/Over/Under/team-goal/clean-sheet/failed-to-score rates, source fixture IDs, H2H details/scope and history cutoff.
- Required label: `HISTORICAL_EMPIRICAL_RATE_NOT_PREDICTION_PROBABILITY`.

### Goal Market Research
- Experimental Poisson forecast, forecast status, model identifier, market estimates and block reasons as emitted by the research engine.
- Preserve fixture context only via exact composite identity.
- Remains research-only, uncalibrated and not betting eligible.

### Market Data Coverage
- Field name, fixture row count, populated count, unavailable count, not-validated/not-graded count and coverage percentage.
- This is syntactic population coverage; it does not establish source correctness, temporal validity or model usefulness.

### Archive Field Coverage
- Raw source field, count with a value, total archive rows, coverage percentage and interpretation.
- Presence only; never treat as proof of semantic correctness or point-in-time availability.

### Definitions & Governance
- Must carry core definitions, supported/unsupported markets, chronology, exact identity, risk/confidence distinction, research/production separation and release eligibility.
- This sheet should reference this versioned dictionary and template version.

## 6. Risk versus confidence

Evidence risk is an evidence-completeness measure. It is not the probability of a team losing, the probability a prediction is wrong, or an inverse confidence score unless a separately validated mapping is formally approved.

Prediction confidence is not currently a validated report field. Do not create a numeric confidence percentage by simply calculating 100 minus evidence risk. A future confidence measure requires a documented method, market-specific chronological out-of-fold evaluation, reliability/calibration analysis, uncertainty intervals and governance approval.

## 7. Historical performance and market integrity

No historical accuracy, ROI, yield, expected value, A+ grade or bet selection may be claimed without:
1. Immutable prediction made before kickoff, with model version and market.
2. Exact fixture and team identity.
3. Official final score and match status.
4. Versioned market-specific settlement rules.
5. Contemporaneous bookmaker odds and timestamp for value/ROI claims.
6. Chronological out-of-fold evaluation and calibration diagnostics.
7. Sufficient sample size, confidence intervals and league baseline comparison.

Do not reconstruct historical predictions from final results. Do not use actual-match xG or post-kickoff statistics as pre-match features.
